# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients — business owners and decision-makers looking to hire a web design & development agency for their project.

## Product Purpose

PixelCraft Studio is a landing page that persuades visitors to contact the agency and hire them for web design, development, and digital projects.

## Positioning

A full-service web agency that designs, develops, and optimizes high-performing websites — with a direct, no-fluff approach focused on conversion and results.

## Operating Context

Single-page landing site with scrollable sections: hero, services, about, portfolio, testimonials, and CTA. Served as a static Vite/React build.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Concept/demo project — content, client names, and stats are placeholders
- Built with React 19 + Vite, CSS Modules, Bootstrap Icons, Google Fonts (Bricolage Grotesque, IBM Plex Sans, JetBrains Mono)
- Light/dark theme toggle
- Responsive design
- Scroll-reveal animations via custom ScrollReveal component

## Brand Commitments

Name: PixelCraft Studio
Tagline: "We build websites that convert."
Voice: Direct, confident, results-oriented. No fluff.

## Evidence on Hand

Placeholder content: fake client names (Acme Co., Lunar Labs, etc.), fabricated stats (200+ projects, 98% uptime), generic tech badges. No real assets or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Conversion-first: every element should guide the visitor toward reaching out.
2. Show, don't tell: portfolio and proof carry more weight than claims.
3. Confidence through craft: the site itself is the first portfolio piece.
